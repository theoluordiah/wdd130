White Water Rafting Trips

Find the trip that matches your sense of adventure. Whether you are new to the river or a seasoned paddler, there is an expedition waiting for you.

The Gauntlet

Class IV-V | Full Day | 12 miles

Our most intense expedition. The Gauntlet pushes teams through relentless Class IV and V rapids with huge drops and technical maneuvers. Experienced guides and strict safety standards make this thrilling run both exciting and secure. Boxed lunch, helmet cam footage, and a post-trip bonfire are included.

Scenic Gulch

Class III | Half Day | 6 miles

A balanced mix of splashy rapids and calm, breathtaking scenery. Scenic Gulch is our most popular trip, great for families and groups who want the adrenaline rush together with time to soak in towering canyon walls and wildlife along the riverbank.

The Easy Rider

Class I-II | Half Day | 4 miles

Our gentlest offering, ideal for first-timers, younger paddlers, and anyone who wants to relax and take in the scenery. With only gentle ripples and plenty of smooth water, the Easy Rider is a confidence-building float that still delivers the beauty of the river.
